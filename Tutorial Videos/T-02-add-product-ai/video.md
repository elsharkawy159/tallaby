The chooser, the form, the bottom bar and the products list are the ones rebuilt for T-01. Every label in the video is the string from `ar.json`.

**Where the real page differs from the outline in TUTORIALS.md §11** ("Choose 'with AI' → add photos → the fields fill in Arabic and English → review → publish"):

- The seller does not only add photos. The AI screen is one message box with a checklist, and the send button stays off until the message describes the product, has at least one photo and **states the price** (`checkComposer`: description, then photo, then price). A PDF can stand in for all three. So step 2 is "write the description and the price" and step 3 is "attach the photos and send".
- **The AI does not set the price or the stock.** The price in the form is the one the seller wrote in the message (the progress step "ضبط السعر والخصم" reads it). The quantity keeps the form's default (12), and the banner asks for it with the chip "أضف الكمية المتاحة". The video shows that chip and does not say or show the AI choosing a price or a quantity.
- The fields do not fill in on the form in front of the seller. The page first shows the seller's message and a progress card with nine steps; the form opens afterwards, already filled, with a banner that says what to check and has "تراجع" (undo).
- The form opens by itself about 2 seconds after the card finishes; the button "مراجعة المنتج" does the same. The video taps the button.
- "نشر" is in a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen. That is under the YouTube Shorts buttons, so the video lifts the screen until the bar sits at y ≈ 1090 (as in T-01). The white band under the bar at that moment is not part of the dashboard.

**Conditions on the real page (the video shows the normal, available state):**

- The AI card is active only when AI is configured on the server (`aiConfigured()`); otherwise the card reads "غير متاح حاليًا" and cannot be tapped.
- There is a daily cap per seller: near the end the page shows "متبقٍ لك {count} مرات اليوم", and at zero "استنفدت مرات الذكاء الاصطناعي لهذا اليوم. حاول غدًا." and the send button is off. A seller with no AI credit left gets "استخدمت رصيد الذكاء الاصطناعي…".
- Limits: up to 4 photos (JPG, PNG, WebP), one PDF of at most 4 MB and 5 pages, 1,500 characters of text.
- When a saved draft already has values, the AI fills only the empty fields unless "استبدل ما كتبته" is ticked. The video starts from an empty form.

**Simplified or not shown:**

- The phone's own file picker (after tapping "إرفاق صور") is not shown; the three photos appear in the box straight away.
- The wait for the answer is about 1 second in the video; the page says it can take up to a minute. The steps tick every 0.3 s (0.32 s on the page).
- What the model writes differs every time. The title, the two-paragraph description, the four highlights, the English name "Handmade Ceramic Mug", the category "أكواب ومجّات" (shown as a Tallaby category, "فئة طلبي · أكواب ومجّات") and the missing-field chip are an example of a normal answer, not a recorded one.
- "جرّب مثالًا", a PDF, customer reviews in the message and the "تراجع" button are on screen but not used.
- While the steps tick, the page is scrolled so the step being written stays in the safe area, so the card's header (percent and the current step's name) is off the top for about 2 seconds.
- The photo counter in the form reads "8 / 3" in Arabic, as on the real page (see T-01).
