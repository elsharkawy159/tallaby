Every label in the video is the string from `ar.json`. The form and the products list are T-01's rebuild of the same pages.

**How the real flow works (single link):** the card "رابط المنتج" opens `?method=url`. One link in the field and "جلب المنتج" calls `/api/fetch-product` twice (Arabic and English), copies the page's photos into the store's media, fills the product form and switches to it (`?method=manual`). It fills: name and short description in both languages, features, photos, price, quantity 25, shipping defaults, and a Tallaby category when the product's name matches one. Then the seller reviews and presses "نشر" as in T-01.

**Which links work:** the code has no list of allowed sites. It reads any `https` product page through its public product data (JSON-LD, Open Graph and price meta tags), with extra reading rules for Amazon pages. A page that does not publish this data fills less, or fails with "فشل جلب بيانات المنتج". The link on screen is a made-up address, not a real store.

**Where the real page differs from the outline in TUTORIALS.md §11** ("Choose 'from a link' → paste the product's link → the fields and photos fill in → review → publish"):

- The card is named "رابط المنتج", not "from a link".
- Pasting does not start the fetch on this screen: the seller presses "جلب المنتج". So "the fields and photos fill in" became the step "اضغط «جلب المنتج»". (Only the form's own "استيراد من رابط" dialog fetches on paste; the video does not use it.)
- The same field takes up to 25 links and then opens a list instead of the form. The video shows one link only.
- The dashboard path is the same page with `?method=url`.

**Simplified or not shown:**

- The phone's own paste menu and keyboard are not shown; the link appears in the field at once. The page is scrolled up so the field and the button sit in the safe area, as it does when the keyboard opens. On the real page the field is focused as soon as the screen opens.
- After the fetch the real page shows two messages together: "تم استيراد 3 صور إلى الوسائط" behind and "تم جلب تفاصيل المنتج بالإنجليزية والعربية. راجعها وعدّلها قبل الحفظ." in front. Only the front one is drawn. It sits at the bottom of the screen like the real one, so the Shorts buttons cover part of it.
- The category shown ("أكواب", "فئة طلبي · أدوات المائدة › أكواب") is a made-up example of a matched Tallaby category; the real names come from Tallaby's category list. When no category matches, the field stays empty, the bar shows "حقل مطلوب متبقٍ" and the seller chooses one before publishing. The video does not show that case.
- "مسح" (clear draft) is drawn in the form's header from the start; on the real page it appears a moment after the form is filled (autosave).
- "نشر" is in a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen, so the video lifts the screen until the bar sits at y ≈ 1090, as in T-01. The white band under the bar at that moment is not part of the dashboard.
- The photo counter reads "8 / 3" in Arabic, as in T-01 (how the real markup renders in a right-to-left line).
