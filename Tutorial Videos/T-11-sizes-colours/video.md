**How the real editor behaves (shown as in the code):**

- The empty state offers "+ اللون", "+ المقاس" and "+ خيار آخر" (`variants-editor.tsx:392`). "إضافة خيار آخر" adds the first kind not used yet from color → size → material, so after "المقاس" it adds "اللون" (`DEFAULT_KINDS`, `addType`). At most 3 options.
- Suggestions: sizes XS…3XL; colours are the first 8 of 15 not taken yet, so "بني" and "وردي" come in as black and white are picked. A colour value gets its swatch from the suggestion.
- The combinations are built as soon as an option has a value. Every new combination takes the product's price (299), the first is the default one ("افتراضي") and keeps the product's quantity (20) and photos; the others start at 0 with no photo. The bulk box shows from 2 combinations; while only one option has values its first chip reads "الكل", and once both have values the rows read "كل المقاس" and "كل اللون" (`allOf`).
- "تطبيق على الكل (6)" sets the stock of all six (the default one through the product's quantity), empties the field and shows "تم تحديث 6 تركيبات" (`applyBulk`).
- With combinations, "الكمية المتاحة" becomes the read-only "إجمالي المخزون" (20, then 60) with "مجموع كميات … عدّل كمية كل تركيبة بالأسفل." (above the part in view).
- On the edit page the bar's button is "حفظ" ("جارٍ الحفظ..." while saving); saving goes to the products list and shows "تم تحديث المنتج بنجاح!" with "عرض" (`handleUpdate`, `showProductSavedToast`).
