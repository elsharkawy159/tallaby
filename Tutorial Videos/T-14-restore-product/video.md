**Source files checked (2026-11-18, `main` at `e83777bf`):** `apps/dashboard/app/[locale]/(main)/stores/[storeId]/products/trash/{page.tsx, trash.client.tsx, trash.server.ts}`, `packages/lib/src/products/{trash.server.ts, trash.lib.ts}`, `apps/ecommerce/vercel.json`, `apps/dashboard/components/dashboard/{Sidebar.tsx, sidebar.config.ts}`, `components/seller-ui/{layout,display,buttons}.tsx`, `packages/ui/src/components/checkbox.tsx`, `lib/i18n/format.ts`, `products/_components/vendor-products.section.tsx` (the list is drawn like T-01's). Every label is the string from `messages/ar.json` (`products.trash`, `products.list`, `nav`).

**What the code does (the facts the voice relies on):**

- Deleting a product moves it to Trash: `status` becomes `draft`, the old status is kept in `trashed_status`, `deleted_at` is set and shoppers' carts drop it (`packages/lib/src/products/trash.server.ts:176-200`).
- Trash keeps it 30 days (`TRASH_RETENTION_DAYS = 30`, `trash.lib.ts:6`; page subtitle "المنتجات المحذوفة تبقى هنا 30 يومًا…"). A daily cron deletes older ones (`purgeExpiredTrash`, `trash.server.ts:299`; `/api/cron/product-trash` at `0 4 * * *`, `apps/ecommerce/vercel.json:12`). A product that orders still point to is kept as a hidden row for order history (`purgeOne`, `trash.server.ts:260-276`).
- Restore puts back the status it had (`restoreProducts`, `trash.server.ts:214-258`), with one exception: a product that was live comes back as a draft when it has no photo or the seller can't publish (`restoredStatus`, `trash.lib.ts:27-39`; `canPublish: seller.status === "approved"`, `apps/dashboard/.../trash/trash.server.ts:248`). So the voice says "رجع زي ما كان" (as the page subtitle says, "ليرجع كما كان"), not "it goes live". The video's product was live with a photo, so it comes back live.
- Restoring a live product can also take a never-published store live (`autoPublishStoreIfReady`, `apps/dashboard/.../trash/trash.server.ts:251`); the video does not touch this.
- The page offers both a row "استرجاع" button and select-then-"استرجاع (n)" (`trash.client.tsx:121-131`, `170-173`); the video uses the selection, which matches "اختر المنتج" and works for several products at once.

**Where the real page differs from the outline in TUTORIALS.md §11** ("Open Trash → restore"):

- The products list has no link to Trash. On a phone the only way is the menu (top bar) → the "المنتجات" group → "سلة المحذوفات" (`sidebar.config.ts:93`). That is step 1.
- The steps are four: open Trash, select, restore, back to "المنتجات" (the page's own back link, `trash.client.tsx:100-105`) to see the product in the list.
- The page's toast is "تم استرجاع منتج واحد"; it has no Undo.

**Simplified or not shown:**

- The menu drawer shows the store pill with a letter instead of a logo, the "لوحة التحكم" link, "البيع", the open "المنتجات" group and "الطلبات"; the rest of the menu (support card, tools) is below the frame and not drawn.
- The product photos are drawn shapes (bag, mug, plates, candle); the list's other three products are T-01's ceramics minus the mug.
- After a restore the real page refreshes the list (`router.refresh()`); the video collapses the row.
- The products list puts the restored product first ("الأحدث أولًا"); the real order depends on its creation date.
