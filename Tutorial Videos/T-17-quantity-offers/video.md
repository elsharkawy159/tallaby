## Notes

**Why this feature and not cross-sell:** TUTORIALS.md §11 lists T-17 as "Quantity offers and cross-sell". They are two cards on the same page with different flows. Quantity offers is the shorter, clearer phone flow (add a tier, which comes pre-filled, change the discount, save) and is fully visible on the product page. Cross-sell needs a product search with a 2-letter minimum and a dropdown, a type and a value, then «إضافة», and its result is spread over the product page, the cart and checkout (plus a store switch and a report at `/orders/cross-sell`). Cross-sell should be its own topic.
