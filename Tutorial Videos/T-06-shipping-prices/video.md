**Where the real page differs from the outline in TUTORIALS.md §11** ("Add an area → set the price → save"):

- Once places are chosen, a new zone's price fills itself with Tallaby's rate for them (Cairo and Giza: 55 × 1.14 rounded down to 5 = 60 جنيه) with the line "سعر طلبي لهذه الأماكن 60 جنيه. استخدمه أو اكتب سعرك." The video then types the seller's own price, 50, so the voice says "خمسين جنيه".
- "حفظ المنطقة" is in the sheet's footer at the bottom of the screen, under the Shorts buttons, so the screen lifts until it sits at y ≈ 1090 for 2.8 s. The white band under the sheet at that moment is not part of the dashboard.
- After saving, the real page shows "تم حفظ المنطقة.", the zone row, step 1's circle turns green and the coverage board "توصّل إلى 2 من 27 محافظة" appears. The message is at the bottom of the screen, where the real one is, so the Shorts buttons cover part of it; the zone and the check are in the safe area.
