**What choosing a template does (checked in the code):** `applyTemplate` merges the template into the builder **drafts** only. A store that is already live keeps its live site until the seller publishes, so the page shows the toast `applied` ("تم تطبيق {name} على المسودة. انشر الموقع ليظهر في متجرك.") and goes to Website › Overview, which then shows "تغييرات غير منشورة". The video shows exactly this case (a store that is already live). It does not show the other two cases: a never-published store with a published product goes live at once (`appliedPublished`), and one with no product stays offline until its first product is published (`appliedNeedsProduct`, with an "إضافة منتج" action). The voice says only that the template is in the draft and is not shown to customers until the seller publishes.

**Where the real page differs from the topic row in TUTORIALS.md §11** ("Open Templates → preview one → choose it"):

- Choosing takes two taps: "استخدام القالب" in the preview, then "استخدام القالب" again in the confirm sheet ("استخدام فخار؟"). So the video has four steps.
- On a phone the tile itself opens the preview dialog; the tile's "معاينة" button opens the full-page preview in a new tab instead. The video taps the tile.
- "استخدام القالب" in the preview and in the confirm sheet sit at the bottom of the screen, under the YouTube Shorts buttons, so the video lifts the screen (about 2 s each) until the button sits at y ≈ 1090. The white band under the screen at those moments is not part of the dashboard.
- After the confirm, the real page leaves the gallery for Website › Overview (`router.push`); the toast is at the bottom of the screen, where the real one is, so the Shorts buttons cover part of it. The box "تغييرات غير منشورة" and the check are in the safe area.
