**Where the real flow differs from the outline in TUTORIALS.md §11** ("Open the editor → change a title and a photo → Publish"):

- Changing a title on a phone takes two taps before typing: the title on the page, then "تعديل" in the action bar that opens the sheet. So the title is two steps (select, then type) and the video has five steps, not three.
- Changing a photo goes through the media library: "تعديل" → "اختيار من المكتبة" → pick a photo → "استخدام الصورة". On a phone a photo is picked with a tap and then the button (a double tap also picks, but it is not shown).
- "نشر" publishes at once: there is no confirmation dialog. It saves the draft first, then shows "تم النشر (الإصدار N). يظهر الآن في متجرك." The button is disabled when there is nothing new to publish, so it is dim until the first edit.
- Edits are saved as drafts by themselves (the cloud icon in the top bar; on a phone only the icon shows). The live site changes only on "نشر" (CLAUDE.md rule 12); the voice says this in step 5.
- The action bar and the dialog's "استخدام الصورة" are near the bottom of the screen, under the Shorts buttons, so the video lifts the screen for those two taps (under 1 s each). "نشر" is in the top bar, inside the top 200 px, so step 5 pushes in 1.35× until it sits at y ≈ 270. The white band under the lifted screen is not part of the editor.
- The success message is at the bottom of the screen (Sonner, bottom-centre), where the Shorts buttons cover part of it; the changed photo, title and the check are in the safe area.
