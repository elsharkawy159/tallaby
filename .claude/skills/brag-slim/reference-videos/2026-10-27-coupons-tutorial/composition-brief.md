## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: polished (app-store tutorial)
- Creative direction: calm Egyptian "How do I…?" with numbered gold step badges
- Interpretation: one step per beat, slow enough to read, push-ins on the field being used.
- Angle / Hook / Outro: see `brag-plan.md`.
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, invented stats, "10 ثواني", sale dates, banned phrases (rules §1), card payment, free shipping.

## Audio
- Role: warm bed under one calm voice.
- Music: original numpy synth (`work/music.py`), maqsum doum-tak 108 BPM, D minor pad + Hijaz pluck motif. No bundled CC BY music.
- Treatment: sidechain duck −11 dB under the voice (60 ms attack / 350 ms release), fade in 0.25 s, fade out 1.5 s.
- SFX: synthesised whoosh on every scene change, soft tap on every simulated tap, key ticks on typing, a chime on "تم إنشاء الكوبون" and "تم تطبيق الكوبون", a bell on the logo (`work/sfx_cues.json`).
- Mixed offline into `composition/assets/audio/mix.wav` (`work/mix.py`), loudness-normalised to about −14 LUFS.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle, time-based breathing of the background glow (no waveform visuals).
