# Compare pages research: EasyOrders, Shopify, WooCommerce, ExpandCart, Salla, Zid (2026-11-10)

Raw notes behind `/compare/tallaby-vs-<id>` and `/alternatives/<id>` (SEO_PLAN.md §3, §4, §9.2–9.3, §15 Phase 3). Every page below was read on **2026-11-10** from official pages (the machine's IP is in Egypt). Earlier research: `02-global-competitors.md` and `03-mena-competitors.md` (2026-11-09).

**Marks:** V = the competitor's own product, pricing, help or docs page, read today · V-blog = its own blog · V-roadmap = its own public roadmap · V-dev = a gateway's or carrier's own docs (Paymob, Kashier), not the platform's · T = third party only · U = not verifiable (reason given). Status is the competitor's own side: y = has it, n = doesn't, p = partly / some plans / through apps.

**How this maps to code:** each fact the pages show is one entry in `COMPETITORS[].facts` in `apps/ecommerce/lib/marketing/compare.lib.ts` (`official(status, url)` for V, V-blog and V-roadmap; `thirdParty(...)` for V-dev and T, shown but not counted). U facts are left out, so their rows don't show. Prices are in `COMPETITOR_PRICES` (`apps/ecommerce/lib/marketing/pricing.lib.ts`) with their own URL and date; copy uses only their tokens. Facts older than 90 days fail `compare-facts-age.test.ts`.

**Open points to settle before publishing (owner):**
- EasyOrders: whether cancelled, fake or returned orders are charged is **still U** (no terms page, FAQ or help center; all 404). The page says so in its FAQ.
- Shopify: yearly prices differed by location today ($24/$65/$359 from an Egyptian IP vs $19/$54/$299 elsewhere). The pages quote only the monthly prices ($27/$72/$399), which agree everywhere.
- ExpandCart: COD, fake orders, zones, Arabic admin and AI are U (its help center fails TLS); its Egyptian gateway and carrier names come from a 2021 page (modified 2022), and the cells say so.
- Zid: Egyptian partners are logos only; several zid.eg pages are copies of the Saudi site.
- Salla: not usable for selling from Egypt (Saudi CR or freelance document, Saudi mobile and Saudi bank account). It gets a comparison, never a place on an "alternatives in Egypt" list (§9.3).

### Contradictions and notes

**EasyOrders**
1. The plan bullets say "متجرك يظل مفتوحًا حتى لو لم تجدّد الاشتراك", while the FAQ on the same page says "عند تأخير الدفع، سيتم إيقاف الخدمات مؤقتاً". Both are on /pricing.md today.
2. The blog (2023, modified 2025-01-29) still says **2 cents per order and a $50 VIP plan**. The live pricing is **$0.04 and $100**.
3. Fawry: the 2026 gateway blog and an older YouCan comparison post list Fawry, but the official roadmap still has "ربط بوابة دفع فوري" as an unbuilt merchant request. Don't claim EasyOrders has Fawry.
4. They market "start free" and "no hidden fees", but the AI page also calls the per-order fee a "commission", and a $5 top-up is needed to activate.
5. Charging for cancelled, fake or returned orders is **still unverifiable**: there is no terms page, FAQ page or help center (all 404). Only the 2023 blog describes wallet mechanics. The fee seems to be charged per order received (the 2023 post says $5 lasts 250 orders), but no page says whether cancelled orders are refunded. Mark U.
6. Roadmap (read today): 206 ideas. Abandoned-cart reminders, a two-language store, Excel export, Fawry and customer store credit are waiting in the queue. Deposits, bundles, an official MCP, Meta descriptions for SEO and back-in-stock alerts are planned. Per-product visit stats and customer login are in progress. Done: blog, special-price add-on, compare, offers page, send to carrier from the order page.

**ExpandCart**
1. Trial length: 15 days on the home, store, channels and marketing pages vs 14 days in the pricing FAQ (AR and EN).
2. Templates: "50+" (home) vs "100+" (store page). Products: "unlimited" (store page) vs 100 on Standard (pricing).
3. Free plan: FAQ and buttons mention a free plan or free store, but the cards don't show one. A third-party index (May 2026) shows "free forever" and $29/month, probably USD geo-pricing that couldn't be checked from Egypt (WebFetch refused).
4. Support: the FAQ says live chat from the dashboard, but the table marks chat support ✗ on Standard.
5. Staleness: the pricing pages were last modified 2023-12-11, the footer says ©2024, the Egypt partner logos are from a 2021 page (modified 2022), and the help center is broken (TLS error, Crisp 402). Every gateway and carrier name except PayPal, PayTabs, Aramex, 2checkout, CashU and Fawaterak comes only from that dated page.
6. The pricing page labels Ultimate "الأكثر مبيعًا" in the cards. The EN page says "Most Popular" over Ultimate.

    - vs Wix adds: "Is Wix cheaper than Shopify?" · "What does Shopify have that Wix doesn't?" · "How do I transfer my domain from Wix to Shopify?" · "Can I use my Wix domain with Shopify without transferring it?"

#### How the pages date and source their claims (worth copying)
- The table carries a dated note: "as of October 2026", with the competitor's facts "accessed October 13, 2026" (vs Woo, vs BigCommerce) or "October 23, 2026" (vs Wix).
- Numbered footnotes link to the **rival's own pricing and help pages** and to Shopify's pricing page. Example: "Plan prices vary by region... card rates and fees shown are US rates". Shopify's prices are noted as "update from Shopify's plan data", so they are pulled live.
- Shopify's own performance claims are labelled as commissioned: "Big Three global management consulting company", "commissioned from a leading independent consulting firm... All values are averages", and EY's "commissioned by Shopify".
- Some numbers are inconsistent on the BigCommerce page: "31%" vs "up to 33%" TCO, and partner/app counts that mix 2,380, 21,000+ and 8,300+.
- The vs Woo page has a stray "*" with no matching footnote.

#### Lessons for Tallaby's pages
- Use a claim H1 plus four number-led reasons.
- Keep a dated table whose footnotes cite the rival's official pages.
- Add a migration block and a FAQ written to match search queries.
- Repeat CTAs throughout.
- Use a localized canonical and hreflang. Shopify ships Arabic `/eg/` versions of these pages, so Tallaby's Arabic "vs Shopify" page competes with Shopify's own Arabic page.

**Shopify**
1. The largest ecosystem: 21,000+ apps and 24 free plus 1,345 paid themes. Official Google and Meta catalog apps with thousands of reviews.

### Contradictions and caveats

1. **Salla, countries:** the FAQ says "متاحة في السعودية وفي الإمارات وقريبًا الخليج كامل", but the footer "مدعوم في" lists six countries (KSA, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman), and help has verification guides for Oman, Qatar and Bahrain.
2. **Salla, merchant count:** the page meta says "60,000" while the page body says "68,000".
3. **Salla, monthly prices:** the static HTML shows only yearly prices (and a garbled "11,88", which is probably the crossed-out 1,188). The monthly 99/299 appear only in the JS module and in a help article marked "قديمة" (old) but updated 22 Aug 2026. The iOS app charges 129/389.
4. **Salla, Basic payments:** Basic gets bank transfer and COD only. Online payment needs Plus or higher, and the ChatGPT connection also needs a paid plan.
5. **Zid, copied pages:** zid.eg/ar/solutions/payments, /marketing-tools, /zid-voice and /themes are text-identical to zid.sa (`diff` showed no difference). The zid.eg shipping page says "تغطية كاملة لكل مناطق المملكة العربية السعودية" with SAR rates. The zid.eg payments page shows Zid Pay, نفاذ, SAR and Riyad Bank. The Zid Voice demo is priced in ر.س.
6. **Zid, e-store page (zid.eg):** it says "وصل منتجاتك لكل أنحاء المملكة", uses the old plan names "لايت، النمو، والباقة الاحترافية", and gives support hours of Sunday to Thursday, 9 to 5. The zid.eg home says 24/7, and the pricing page calls the first paid plan "الانطلاقة / Rise".
7. **Zid, sales commission:** the Saudi FAQ excludes commission only for "لايت والنمو والاحترافية". Starter is not addressed.
8. **Zid, Egypt Starter vs Saudi Starter:** the Saudi Starter lists "اضافة منتجاتك في سوق مزيد امام 5 مليون عميل". The Egypt Starter does not.
9. **Zid, EGP prices:** they are exactly the SAR prices × 11.4 (99 × 11.4 = 1,128.60), so they are a currency conversion, not separate Egypt pricing.
10. **Zid, Growth annual total:** the Saudi page JSON totals differ ("2,990" AR vs "2,988" EN in the earlier read). This was not re-checked in detail today.
11. **Zid, Egyptian partners:** Paymob, Valu, Sympl and Bosta appear only as logos. The help center payment-providers article names Tap, Tabby and Tamara (Saudi stores) and no Egyptian gateway.
